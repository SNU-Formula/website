ABOUT SNU FORMULA

WE ARE
SNU FORMULA.

SNU FORMULA는 *SNU Mobility Renovatio의 산하 모터스포츠 팀입니다.SNU FORMULA is a motorsport team under *SNU Mobility Renovatio.

*SNU Mobility Renovatio는 서울대학교 산학협력공학인재지원센터(센터장: 재료공학부 곽승엽 교수)가 주관하는 실전형 모터스포츠 모빌리티 인재 양성 프로젝트입니다.*SNU Mobility Renovatio is a hands-on motorsport and mobility talent development project led by Seoul National University’s University–Industry Liaison Center for Engineers (Director: Professor Seung Yeop Kwak, Department of Materials Science and Engineering).

FORMULA SAE · FORMULA STUDENT · FSG

Formula SAE와 Formula Student는 대학생 팀이 포뮬러형 차량을 직접 설계·제작·시험하고 전문가의 평가를 받는 국제 공학 경진대회입니다. Formula SAE and Formula Student are international engineering competitions in which university teams design, build, and test formula-style vehicles and present their work to expert judges.

주행 기록만 겨루는 대회가 아닙니다. 차량의 설계 개념부터 제작, 원가, 사업성, 신뢰성까지 종합적으로 평가합니다. This is more than a race against the clock. Teams present and defend their vehicles’ design, manufacturing, cost, business viability, and reliability.
