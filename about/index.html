ABOUT SNU FORMULA

WE ARE
SNU FORMULA.

SNU FORMULA는 서울대학교 학생들이 EV race car라는 하나의 복합 시스템을 함께 설계하고 증명하는 Formula Student Team입니다. SNU FORMULA is a Formula Student team where Seoul National University students engineer and prove one complex EV race car.

FORMULA SAE · FORMULA STUDENT · FSG

Formula SAE와 Formula Student는 대학생이 formula-style Vehicle을 직접 설계·제작·시험하고, 그 결과를 전문가 앞에서 증명하는 국제 engineering competition 분야입니다. Formula SAE and Formula Student are international engineering competitions where university teams design, build, test, and defend their own formula-style vehicles.

단순한 lap-time contest가 아닙니다. Team은 Vehicle의 concept부터 manufacturing, cost, business, reliability까지 하나의 product로 설명합니다. This is more than a lap-time contest. Teams present the vehicle as a complete product—from concept and manufacturing to cost, business, and reliability.

GERMANY

Formula Student Germany는 Hockenheim에서 열리는 대표적인 Formula Student competition입니다. SNU FORMULA는 2028년 참가를 목표로 합니다. Formula Student Germany is a leading Formula Student competition held at Hockenheim. SNU FORMULA is targeting the 2028 event.
