PARTNERSHIP MODEL

지원에서
engineering evidence까지. FROM SUPPORT
TO EVIDENCE.

Partnership는 logo placement로 끝나지 않습니다. 기술 목표를 맞추고, vehicle에 적용하고, test result와 record로 다시 연결하는 workflow를 지향합니다. Partnership goes beyond logo placement. We align technical goals, apply capability to the vehicle, test it, and close the loop with records.

01

ALIGN

공동 목표와 공개 범위, deliverable을 정의합니다.Define shared goals, disclosure boundaries, and deliverables.

02

BUILD

기술, 소재, 제작 capability를 vehicle에 적용합니다.Apply technology, material, and manufacturing capability.

03

TEST

명확한 criteria와 measurement로 결과를 확인합니다.Evaluate outcomes with explicit criteria and measurements.

04

RECORD

승인된 evidence와 learning을 다음 iteration에 남깁니다.Carry approved evidence and learning into the next iteration.
