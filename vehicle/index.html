SNU FORMULA가 만들고자 하는 Vehicle의 핵심 방향입니다. 상세 specification보다 팀이 달성하려는 기술적 목표를 보여드립니다. These are the four technical directions behind the SNU FORMULA vehicle—focused on intent rather than unpublished specifications.

01 / PERFORMANCE

USABLE
PERFORMANCE.

단일 peak 수치보다 Formula Student course 전체에서 driver가 활용할 수 있는 performance를 목표로 합니다.Target performance the driver can use across a complete Formula Student course—not an isolated peak.

  • Lap-time-led decisions
  • Responsive vehicle behavior
  • Event-wide energy use
02 / LIGHTWEIGHT

MASS WITH
PURPOSE.

무조건 가벼운 Vehicle이 아니라 strength, packaging, serviceability를 함께 만족하는 경량화를 추구합니다.Pursue lightweight design that also protects strength, packaging, and serviceability.

  • Compact packaging
  • Mass-aware structure
  • Serviceable integration
03 / RELIABILITY

BUILT TO
FINISH.

Scrutineering 통과와 Endurance 완주를 기준으로 safety와 reliability를 함께 설계합니다.Engineer safety and reliability around clearing scrutineering and completing Endurance.

  • Safety by design
  • Consistent thermal behavior
  • Finish before peak

DECISION.

  • Measurable vehicle behavior
  • Control-ready data
  • AS-ready direction
